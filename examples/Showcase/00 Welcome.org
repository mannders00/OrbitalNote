* TODO Your notes, with room to think
This is a real folder of editable Org files. Open it as a workspace, or copy it
somewhere of your own first. The examples work offline, including diagrams,
equations, and the image stored in the attachments folder.

Try the reading/edit button to move between rendered notes and their Org source.
Changes made through heading menus are source edits, with undo available.

** Take a two-minute tour
- Open [[file:01 Rich notes.org][Rich notes]] for images, a Mermaid diagram, math, and tables.
- Open [[file:02 Fieldwork.org][Fieldwork]] for tasks, properties, tags, and planning.
- Open [[file:03 Research.org][Research]] for equations, code, and linked notes.
- Use Agenda → + Filter to build a view, name it, and save it.
- Drag tabs to reorder them; the blue marker shows the insertion point.
- Drag a tab toward a document edge to create a split.
- Try Settings → Appearance, or assign shortcuts to heading/insertion commands.

** Heading controls
Hover near a heading to reveal its fold control and kebab menu. The menu includes
task editing, task-state toggling, tags, properties, priority, and outline actions.
Click a red TODO or NEXT label to complete the task; click green DONE to reopen it.

** About this sample
Dates are fixed to the week of September 28, 2026 so the files remain deterministic.
Choose All tasks in the agenda or navigate the calendar to that week.
Attachments are ordinary local files. Current encrypted Sync transfers Org notes,
not attachments. Mermaid/KaTeX render in reading view; their source remains editable.
