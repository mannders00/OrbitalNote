** Put the reasoning in the note
A simple model helps us decide what to work on: \(V = \frac{I \times C}{E}\),
where impact and confidence are weighed against effort.

#+begin_src latex
\operatorname{priority} = \frac{\operatorname{impact}\times\operatorname{confidence}}{\operatorname{effort}}
#+end_src

| Idea                 | Impact | Confidence | Effort |
|----------------------+--------+------------+--------|
| A clearer first run  |      8 |       0.90 |      3 |
| Faster task capture  |      7 |       0.85 |      2 |
| Better review notes  |      5 |       0.95 |      1 |
