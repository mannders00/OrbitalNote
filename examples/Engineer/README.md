# A backend, a dog, and the rest of Thursday

The real Org files behind the website's desktop and mobile workflow screenshots.
Open a copy of this folder in OrbitalNote. No account or imports are needed.

- **Backend.org:** Dispatch, a small Go + Postgres support-ticket API. Design notes,
  a handler sketch, checklists, and a week of scheduled engineering work.
- **Worker capacity.org:** a scheduled TODO with tags, Little's law in LaTeX,
  a Mermaid service diagram, and a capacity-check checklist.
- **Request timing.org:** a short Go instrumentation sketch for queue wait and
  processed-job metrics.
- **Life.org:** dog walks, exercise, rent, groceries, cooking, and taking out the trash.

The screenshots use **Thursday, October 1, 2026 at 10:30 AM**. Navigate to that
week to see the same calendar. The example includes completed morning tasks;
these are fictional demonstration data.

Desktop layout (Systems notebook): week Calendar above a minimal Agenda on the
left, with rendered worker-capacity notes above request-timing source on the right. Mobile: today's Agenda
from these exact same files.
The screenshots are local app captures, not a recording of a live Sync session.
Optional Sync can keep a real workspace shared between desktop and mobile.
