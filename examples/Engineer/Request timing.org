* Measure the queue, not just the handler
Keep a histogram of queue wait time beside request latency.

#+begin_src go
wait := time.Since(job.EnqueuedAt)
queueWait.Observe(wait.Seconds())
err := worker.Process(ctx, job)
jobsProcessed.WithLabelValues(status(err)).Inc()
