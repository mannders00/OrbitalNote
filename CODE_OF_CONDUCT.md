# Community conduct

OrbitalNote welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be kind, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing workflows, accessibility needs, and levels of experience.
- Keep discussion relevant and constructive. Do not harass, threaten, insult,
  discriminate, or publish someone else's private information.
- Do not post private notes, credentials, or recovery keys in issues or discussions.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock conversations,
or restrict participation. Serious or repeated abuse may result in a ban.

Report concerns privately to **matt@masoftware.net**. Reports will be handled with
care and shared only as needed to investigate and respond. If a concern involves
a maintainer, GitHub's abuse-reporting tools are also available.
